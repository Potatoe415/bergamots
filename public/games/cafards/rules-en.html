Rules

Contents

112 vegetable cards: 28 tomatoes, 28 lettuces, 28 peppers, 28 cauliflowers. 16 taboo cards: 4 per vegetable.

Goal

Players play cards to the center and speak aloud. Some situations force you to lie. If a taboo card appears, the player must shout COCKROACH and that vegetable becomes taboo.

Two piles

Whenever a taboo card appears, play switches to the other pile. The two visible taboo cards can make two vegetables taboo at the same time.

Mistake

If a player makes a wrong call, hesitates too long, or slips up, they collect every card from both piles.

Player display

At the bottom of the screen, each colored circle represents a player and shows how many cards they have left.

Win

The first player to get rid of all cards wins immediately.
