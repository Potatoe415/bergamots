Goal of the game

Read the short mystery, imagine what could have happened, then reveal the full explanation.

How to play

  • Read the short story shown on screen.
  • Think, alone or with others, about what could have led to this situation.
  • Tap "REVEAL" to discover the full story and see if you guessed right.
  • Use the ← and → arrows to move to another story.

Group tip

One person reads the short story and keeps the full explanation secret; everyone else asks yes/no questions to guess what happened, before revealing the answer.

Language

Change the stories' language from Settings, top right.
