Goal of the game

Make your team guess the main word without saying any of the taboo words shown under it.

Setup

  • Form at least two teams.
  • Choose a time limit per turn (for example 60 seconds).
  • Get something to track scores.

Turn sequence

  • One player draws a card and reads it silently.
  • They describe the main word without using the taboo words.
  • If a taboo word is spoken, the card is lost and you move to the next one.

Scoring

Each correctly guessed word scores 1 point. Cards where a taboo word is spoken score 0.
