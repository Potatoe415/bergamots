Goal of the game

Make your team guess as many cards as possible by following the rules of the selected deck.

Setup

  • Pick a deck on the setup screen.
  • Set the round timer in seconds.
  • Form at least two teams.

Turn sequence

  • Start the timer and reveal the first card.
  • Make your team guess the card while respecting the deck rules (miming, forbidden words, etc.).
  • Use “Found” to score a point, “Pass” to skip a card.

End of the round

When time is up, count the points scored in this round. Switch teams and start a new round.
