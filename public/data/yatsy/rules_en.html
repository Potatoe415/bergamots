Goal of the game

Each turn, roll 5 dice (up to 3 times) to build the best combination you can, then score it into an empty category. Whoever has the highest total across every category wins.

A turn

  • First roll: all 5 dice are rolled.
  • Between rolls, keep the dice you want and reroll the rest (up to 2 more rerolls).
  • After the 3rd roll (or sooner if you want), score your result into any still-empty category.

Upper section

Ones through Sixes: score the sum of the dice showing that value. A 35 point bonus is awarded if the upper section total reaches 63 points or more.

Lower section

Full House (30), Four of a Kind (40), Large Straight (40) and Yatzy (50, five matching dice) score a fixed value. Minimum and Maximum score the sum of all 5 dice. A few extra categories (Small Straight, 3 Dice Same, Luck) can be turned on from Settings.

End of the game

The game ends once every category is filled in. Add up the scores: the highest total wins the game.
