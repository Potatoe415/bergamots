Goal of the game

Make your team guess a word by drawing it, without using any letters or numbers.

Setup

  • Form teams of at least 2 players.
  • Choose a time limit per round (for example 60 seconds).
  • Prepare paper or a whiteboard for drawing.

On your turn

  • Draw a word using the “Next word” button.
  • Draw only: no letters, no numbers, no obvious symbols.
  • Your team may shout out as many guesses as they want.

Scoring

If your team finds the word before time runs out, score 1 point. Otherwise, you score 0.
