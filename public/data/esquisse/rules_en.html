Goal of the game

Collectively draw a scene or idea so that it can be guessed at the end of the round.

Setup

  • Each player has a sheet of paper and a pen.
  • Choose how many rounds you want to play.
  • Turn the timer on or off depending on your group.

Turn sequence

  • Draw a word with the “Next word” button.
  • The first player starts drawing a scene for that word.
  • At each step, the sheet moves to the next player, who adds to the drawing.

End of the round

Reveal the original word and compare it to the final drawing. Laugh, then start a new round.
